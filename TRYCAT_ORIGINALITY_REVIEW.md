# TRYCAT™ Originality and Interaction Review

## Intent

TRYCAT is designed as an **editorial strategic instrument**, not a generic consultancy, SaaS, AI, or pet-brand site. Its visual language is anchored in the supplied approved three-cat artwork and the methodological relationship between **Business**, **System**, and **Human**. The site does not introduce alternate cat drawings, character props, stock photography, bento-card grids, gradient-led hero styling, or decorative 3D objects.

## Proprietary Visual Grammar

| TRYCAT principle | Implemented expression | Why it is specific to TRYCAT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three perspectives | The master artwork, perspective hotspots, diagnostic labels, triad evidence line, and intersection diagram all repeat Business / System / Human. | The three cats are a working methodology rather than a logo decoration. |
| Observation | The master-artwork frame uses a cursor-responsive observation field and perspective-specific labels: **Observe**, **Connect**, and **Design**. | Attention is framed as diagnosis, not mascot play. |
| Intersection | Thin rails, crosshairs, three rings, node lines, directional indices, and constrained grids recur throughout the page. | Every geometric mark represents a relationship, a condition, or a decision point. |
| Movement | The five-stage sequence moves from Diagnose through Deliver, culminating in Clarity → Decision → Transformation. | Movement is an explicit methodological outcome, not a generic animation motif. |
| Editorial hierarchy | Oversized neo-grotesk headlines, mono operational labels, measured negative space, and horizontal evidence rows create the reading rhythm. | The experience reads like a field report and an interactive operating system rather than a landing-page template. |
| Colour semantics | Carbon structures the interface, ivory creates reading space, grey denotes Business, ginger identifies Human/action, and indigo carries strategy/system intelligence. | Colour follows the three-perspective model instead of serving as broad decoration. |

## Master Artwork Safeguards

The supplied three-cat artwork remains the visual source of truth. The site references the approved master asset and compact approved symbol through managed asset URLs. Motion is applied around the artwork using frame transforms, hover hotspots, diagnostic fields, and CSS-only signals; the asset’s silhouette, cat proportions, line quality, and palette are not redrawn or replaced.

## Purposeful Interaction System

| Interaction | Behaviour | Accessibility / restraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cat-perspective hotspot | Hover, focus, and click select Business, System, or Human and update the active methodology lens. | Each hotspot is a labelled button with keyboard focus; there is no autonomous mascot animation. |
| Observation field | Pointer movement introduces a controlled diagnostic light field across the artwork frame. | It is removed for `prefers-reduced-motion`. |
| Perspective switcher | A user can choose any of the three lenses in the methodology panel. | Uses semantic buttons and visible active states. |
| Five-stage journey | Diagnose, Clarify, Decide, Design, and Deliver update a live, editorial detail panel. | The sequence is keyboard operable and communicates status through text, not colour alone. |
| Convergence | The dark end section resolves the three lenses into Clarity, Decision, and Transformation with the approved compact symbol. | It concludes the narrative without introducing unapproved character treatments. |

## Explicit Exclusions

The implementation deliberately avoids glassmorphism, neon/gradient branding, stock imagery, floating 3D objects, animated dashboards, invented testimonials, generic service-card matrices, cute/cartoon cats, and use of the logo as a prop carrier. Rounded surfaces are limited to functional form controls; content is organised through rails, evidence lines, indices, and editorial pacing.

## Verification

Desktop and mobile captures confirm that the original narrative, persistent Book a Call access, master-artwork prominence, methodology journey, and convergence section remain readable at their respective breakpoints. The page uses transform/opacity-oriented motion and a global reduced-motion fallback. The remaining external production checks are recorded in `SECURITY_AUDIT.md` and `todo.md`.
